Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

At Markdownizer, accessible from our extension and website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Markdownizer and how we use it.

Information Collection

We believe in data minimization. Markdownizer is designed so that readable page text is tokenized locally in your browser before conversion.

  • Web Content: To convert a page into Markdown, the extension temporarily accesses the content of the active tab. Readable page text is tokenized locally and is not sent to our servers as readable text.
  • Structural Conversion Data: Markdownizer sends a structural page skeleton to the backend so the service can convert structure into Markdown. This structural payload may still include non-text page elements such as links, image references, and other HTML attributes.
  • Operational Data: We collect limited operational data needed to run, secure, and improve the service. This may include an anonymous user identifier, request size, output size, conversion duration, failure or status information, extraction strategy, and IP address.
  • What We Do Not Store For Analytics: We do not store readable page text, final Markdown output, full page URLs, or raw HTML skeletons in our analytics data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our extension.
  • Measure conversion success, performance, and reliability.
  • Investigate failures, abuse, and suspicious traffic patterns.
  • Improve conversion quality and overall product stability.

Privacy Summary

  • Readable page text never leaves your browser as readable text.
  • Markdownizer sends a structural skeleton for conversion.
  • We keep limited operational metrics so the service can be run safely and improved over time.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

Markdownizer uses page data only to provide its user-facing feature: converting the current page into clean Markdown for your preferred LLM or notes workflow.

  • Allowed use: Page data is used only to provide or improve Markdown conversion and related reliability, abuse prevention, and security functions.
  • Allowed transfer: Structural conversion data is transferred only as needed to provide the conversion service, operate the backend safely, or comply with applicable law.
  • No advertising use: User data is not used or transferred for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • No human review of readable content: We do not allow humans to read readable page text as part of normal operations. Limited exceptions may apply for security, legal compliance, or support situations where a user explicitly consents.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Markdownizer's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.
