A few useful answers.

How do I use Markdownizer?

Open a webpage, start Markdownizer from your browser toolbar, then copy the Markdown into your prompt or download it as a .md file.

Can I use it on a page that needs a login?

Markdownizer works from the page you already have open. It does not give access to content you cannot view or permission to share private material.

Can I save a Markdown file?

Yes. After conversion, copy the result or download it as a .md file.

Does Markdownizer generate the AI response?

No. Markdownizer converts the page; your chosen AI tool answers your prompt. Demo comparisons and responses are illustrative, and results vary by page and app.

Does everything happen in my browser?

No. Readable text is tokenized locally, a structural skeleton is sent for conversion, and the readable Markdown is rebuilt in your browser. The skeleton can include links, image references, and other attributes. Read the privacy policy.

Will every webpage convert perfectly?

Page structure varies. Review the output, especially complex tables, code, or content that has not loaded in your browser.
