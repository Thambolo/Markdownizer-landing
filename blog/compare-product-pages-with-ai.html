Comparison workflow

How to compare product pages with AI using Markdown

Give the comparison a traceable source for every price, feature, date, and unknown.

To compare product pages with AI, capture each page as a separate source, record where and when you viewed it, and ask for a comparison using only details present in those sources. Normalize labels and units carefully, but keep differences visible instead of silently treating them as equivalent. A tidy table helps; it does not make a product claim current or independently verified.

Markdownizer can convert a webpage you have open into Markdown. It is useful for carrying headings, lists, links, and supported tables into your working notes. For the current extraction and export behavior, see the feature guide.

Start with comparable, primary sources

Prefer official product pages for specifications and terms, and distinguish those from independent reviews or marketplace listings. If two pages describe different versions, bundles, regions, or customer plans, they are not necessarily a like-for-like comparison. Note the exact product variant and source URL with each capture.

Check whether the page's content is fully loaded and whether a price refers to a monthly plan, annual billing, a single purchase, or a promotional period. For products with options, separate the base model from optional accessories or service tiers. Do not infer a missing specification from a photograph or from another product in the same line.

Keep the source records separate at first

  1. Open one product page and convert it. Review whether the relevant specifications, included items, exclusions, warranty terms, or return details were captured.
  2. Save the source identity beside the Markdown: page title, manufacturer or seller, URL, visible version or variant, and the date you checked it.
  3. Repeat for the other product rather than blending both pages into one undifferentiated paste.
  4. Compare like with like only after confirming each row has the same meaning, units, and conditions.

If Smart selection misses details lower on a page, full-page capture covers the current page body, but it does not automatically scroll to load every section. Check the output rather than assuming that the full product listing came through. The Smart selection vs. full-page guide explains the difference.

Build a comparison that exposes gaps

An illustrative template, not real product data, might look like this:

| Criterion | Product A source | Product B source | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | [amount, currency, billing period] | [amount, currency, billing period] | Region and date |
| Size or capacity | [quoted value and unit] | [quoted value and unit] | Are units comparable? |
| Included items | [listed items] | [listed items] | Bundle or optional? |
| Warranty | [term and conditions] | [term and conditions] | Same market and seller? |

Do not convert currencies or units without recording how you did it. If the pages disagree, preserve both source statements and ask the AI to flag the discrepancy. If a field is absent, use "not stated in this source," not a guessed value or an empty cell that could mean anything.

Ask a bounded question and verify the result

Be explicit about the allowed evidence: "Compare only the details in the two source notes. Separate direct page statements from your interpretation. Keep currencies and billing periods as shown. Mark missing, conflicting, or ambiguous details; do not fill gaps from memory. End with questions I should confirm with the seller."

This is an illustrative instruction, not a promise about an AI response. Reopen the source for every decision-driving claim, particularly price, availability, compatibility, safety, warranty, and return conditions. A link in Markdown carries a destination but does not mean the AI tool opened it. AI tools also differ in their context limits and browsing or file features.

Review tables, dates, and sharing permissions

Check that values remain under the correct headers, that units travel with numbers, and that notes or footnotes have not become detached from a row. Merged cells, nested tables, and visual groupings may need manual interpretation. Read how to check copied webpage tables before relying on a complex comparison.

Product pages can also contain account-specific links or query parameters. Remove details you do not need before sharing the notes. Review what to remove before sharing webpage content with AI, and check the destination service's privacy and file-handling settings.
