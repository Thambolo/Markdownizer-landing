Research workflow

How to turn a research article into useful AI context

Keep the question, method, evidence, limitations, and citation details connected before asking for an explanation.

To prepare a research article for an AI prompt, bring in the relevant text with its title and citation details, then check that the research question, methods, findings, and limitations are still easy to distinguish. Do not rely on an abstract alone when your question depends on how a result was produced. A conversion can make text easier to organize; it cannot verify the study or supply missing evidence.

For a webpage you can access, Markdownizer can turn the current page into Markdown that you copy or download. The article remains your source to check. For a starting workflow, see how to convert a webpage for an AI chat.

Keep the research question and study design together

Research claims need context. A result may depend on the population, setting, sample, measurement, comparison group, or period studied. If you copy only a paragraph describing a finding, an AI tool may not have enough source material to explain what the finding does and does not cover.

Before conversion, identify the sections relevant to your task. Depending on the page, these may include the abstract, methods, results, discussion, limitations, and references. A methods section can answer questions that a conclusion cannot: who was included, how an outcome was measured, whether the work was observational, and what comparison was made.

Make a small source record

Keep a short note beside the converted text with details that help you return to the original:

  • Title and authors: Use the page's displayed information rather than guessing missing names.
  • Publication or version: Record the journal, repository, or document version when shown.
  • Date and location: Note the publication date and source URL. Check query parameters in links before sharing them.
  • Your purpose: Write down the exact question so you can omit sections that do not help answer it.

These notes are a navigation aid, not a substitute for a proper citation. If you need a formal reference, verify it using the publication or an approved citation source. A link in Markdown does not mean an AI tool opened or checked that destination.

Convert and inspect the parts that matter

  1. Open the article normally in a supported Chrome or Chromium browser and let the sections you need load. Markdownizer does not bypass a login, access restriction, or an unloaded section.
  2. Choose the capture scope. Smart selection is the default and looks for an article or main area before falling back to the body. If important material is outside that area, consider full-page capture for the current conversion. It captures the page body, not content that has not loaded.
  3. Review the output after conversion. Check headings, numbered steps, tables, links, and any footnotes or caveats that your question depends on. The selection preview highlights webpage content; it is not a Markdown editor or rendered Markdown preview.
  4. Copy or download the result and edit it in a separate editor if you need to trim or annotate it.

Ask for explanation, not invented authority

State what the AI should do and how it should handle uncertainty. For example:

Task: Explain the study's research question, design, main findings, and stated limitations.
Use only the source material below for claims about this study.
Separate the authors' conclusions from your explanation.
If a citation detail or result is not present, say what is missing.

Source:
[Paste the reviewed article text and source record here]

This is an illustrative prompt, not a guaranteed result. If you need to cite a claim, open the original source and verify the wording, table, and bibliographic details yourself. Do not treat generated references as real merely because they look complete. For more on keeping numerical relationships visible, read how to check webpage tables after copying.

Use only the material you are allowed to share

Check publication terms, course rules, workplace requirements, and the destination AI service's privacy settings before pasting material. Markdownizer tokenizes readable text locally, sends a structural skeleton for conversion, and rebuilds readable Markdown in the browser. The skeleton may contain links, image references, and other attributes. Copying the result into an AI service is a separate sharing action. The privacy policy and feature guide describe the product's current data handling and limitations.
